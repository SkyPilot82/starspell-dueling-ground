The truncation law, verbatim for the build card:
  1. Buttons and tags never shrink. Pill width = ceil((textWidth+28)/10)·10, clamped 76–128, font fixed 10 pt. The 7.5→5.5 pt strip loop and the 9.5→7 pt sheet loop die.
  2. Names never shrink. Fixed 13 pt (page rows) / 13 pt (sheet rows) / 10 pt (fold pills), the shipped ellipsis-while-loop at 168 / 128 / 78 units.
  3. Subs never shrink. Fixed 9.5 pt, same clip as their name.
  4. Page floor: nothing interactive under 10 pt, nothing readable under 8.5 pt — today’s floors are 5.5 pt (interactive!) and 6.5–7 pt. The big gold doors keep their fit-to-width loops (long de/pt labels genuinely need them) with the floor raised 7 → 9 pt.
  5. Checked in all five languages — the harness pins es OTRA VEZ and de NOCHMAL (the widest words) at full 10 pt in their buckets, and a 24-char name whole at 13 pt.

IV · TWO SURFACES, ONE ANATOMY — WHY THE SHEET SURVIVES

Keep both surfaces, unify the row. The page seats the few — ranked picks, zero taps away. The sheet holds the many — the full friends roll, the deep recent list, add-by-name, the app invite. One row builder means one truncation law everywhere; merging them entirely would either scroll the scene (new machinery, new laws) or cap the roll at what the page can seat. The “› ALL MAGES” door survives only in the fold — on the open page the gold CHALLENGE A FRIEND door already is the sheet’s front door, and two doors to one room is one too many.

V · TEXTURE — WHAT MAKES A ROW A PERSON (question 3)

A full-size row has room the pills never had. Two textures, both from data the game already holds, zero new synced data:

The rating. Sub-line leads with the mage’s tier glyph + rating — ✦ 1024 · last night. Humans: one registry read per row, ≤5 per page open, cached for the visit, the row dressing itself in place when the number lands (the redress law — nothing reorders). Circle mages: the rival engine already keeps their Elo. Stakes make a rematch worth wanting — “can I take 1210?” is a story; a bare name is not.

The last word (the flourish). Where the ledger already holds it — near-duel notes carry the rival’s lastWord — the sub may whisper it instead of silence: “their last word: EMBER”. Local-only, spotty by nature (live-sky duels don’t note it today), so it is offered as the garnish, never the spine.

VI · THE LAWS THE BUILD INHERITS

  1. safeB anchoring. Doors at safeB−158/−90, whisper/note at safeB−44, formula verbatim (the suites pin it). NEW: the funnel’s three doors join the safeB family (safeB−370/−296/−222) and the offline retry anchors safeB−158 — today both sit at fixed y and open a foot gap on tall phones.
  2. The doorsC contract. Doors live in a container, destroyed + rebuilt on funnel↔duels flips; container children never appear in scene.children.list — suites aim at refs (chWorldB = the sentinel, chFriendB NULL in funnel); every ref nulled in create() because scene instances persist across restarts.
  3. pendC rebuilds, stripC redresses. The replay rows live in pendC (their count is plaque-coupled), so the pendKey gains the shown-row-count + picks component; the fold strip stays built-once. Picks rank once per open; the 1-s tick redresses dots/subs/pills in place and never reorders.
  4. 44-pt tap zones everywhere (rows are 50 tall; pills wear ssHitPad 44).
  5. Five-duel cap pips; an unclaimed summons holds no slot; every row action routes through the cap gate (vsCapSheet) exactly as the chips do today.
  6. Funnel and offline are designed states. Funnel: hero 2.0 + fiction + three safeB-anchored doors — no people invented for a device that knows nobody. Offline: the ghost ledger becomes ghost rows (same anatomy, dimmed, no pills, the shipped status fiction as the sub) + vsGhost line + anchored retry.
  7. The sky is kept. ssSkyWorld versus dress, seed 0x1701, no moon, navy veil, crest fireflies — the hero flex touches scale and anchors only.
  8. Bots indistinguishable. Circle rows byte-dress like human rows; glint, never “online”; no circle/bot/ai word on any surface.
  9. Record shapes kept. pendRows verbatim; the new rosterRows records ship for the suites the way chipRows do; vsGameRows() untouched.
  10. The U+2694 grep gate stands. Zero new strings ×5 — every word on the filled page already ships (vsTonight, vsAgain, vsDuelTag, the presence words, the night-clock, vsGhost). No new art files.
  11. Out of scope, named: the HOME meadow duel strip (game.js ~6150–6239) is a different surface and does not move. Card 03 (the in-battle duel column) is independent — a plaque’s › still enters vsbattle, and any battle doorway this design implies assumes card 03’s bigger-health-bar language once it lands.
